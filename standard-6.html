Professional Experience 1

Standard 6: Teaching

ProfEx 1 reflections and evidence for Standards 2, 3 and 6.

All standardsStandard 2Standard 3Standard 6

Focus

Use effective teaching strategies, subject knowledge and assessment information to engage learners, promote progress and foster critical thinking.

Reflection

I am learning to connect practical work with evidence of students’ thinking. The current-measurement lesson asks students to predict, measure at three positions and compare their findings before the expected pattern is explained. The plan includes safe ammeter modelling and distinct group roles. My register records that Y10 Science 1 completed the measurement task independently on 23 October and that discussions went well.

My mentor teacher’s formal observation of the 21 October circuit lesson records engagement, clear learning objectives and subject expertise. These are useful strengths, but engagement alone does not establish individual understanding. I changed that lesson’s exit ticket to oral responses because of time, so I did not collect a written exit response from every student. Pooled, anonymised worksheet and exercise-book samples are linked below. Their individual pages have not been attributed to either Year 10 group. There is no sample-by-sample class comparison and no inference of whole-class mastery.

Next steps: review the work against the success criteria and annotate specific examples of secure understanding or misconceptions. Use those findings to decide what to revisit. Protect time for a brief individual response and evaluate whether it confirms the understanding seen during practical work.

Year 10 Science 2 completed Lesson 4, the current-measurement practical, on 29 October 2026. Most students worked productively; two students did not complete the task despite reminders. This observation helps identify where more individual checking is needed.

Evidence links
