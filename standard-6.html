Professional Experience 1

Standard 6: Teaching

ProfEx 1 reflections and evidence for Standards 2, 3 and 6.

All standardsStandard 2Standard 3Standard 6

Focus

Use effective teaching strategies, subject knowledge and assessment information to engage learners, promote progress and foster critical thinking.

Reflection

I am learning to connect practical work with evidence of students’ thinking. The current-measurement lesson asks students to predict, measure at three positions and compare their findings before the expected pattern is explained. The plan includes safe ammeter modelling and distinct group roles. My register records that Y10 Science 1 completed the measurement task independently on 23 October and that discussions went well.

My mentor teacher’s formal observation of the 21 October circuit lesson records engagement, clear learning objectives and subject expertise. These are useful strengths, but engagement alone does not establish individual understanding. I changed that lesson’s exit ticket to oral responses because of time, so I did not collect a written exit response from every student. Year 10 worksheet and exercise-book samples are linked below.

In the November electricity generation practical, one student worksheet records zero with the motor still and a higher reading when the shaft was spun faster. The student also called the meter an ammeter. The observations show that the student noticed the relationship between movement and the reading, but the explanation does not yet distinguish current from voltage. That is a more useful finding than simply recording that the worksheet was completed.

On the end-of-placement questionnaire, 25 of 28 respondents agreed or strongly agreed that the lessons helped them understand circuits, and all 28 agreed or strongly agreed that they got help when they struggled. The survey tells me how students experienced the teaching. I read it alongside the practical work, which shows what particular students observed and where their explanations were still developing.

Next steps: in future practical lessons, protect time for a brief individual explanation. For a generator investigation, I would ask students to state what the meter measured and explain how the reading changed when they spun the shaft faster.

Key Teaching Tasks

KTT 6: Slides, circuit diagrams and visual representations support explanations and practical instructions.

KTT 7: My plans connect Science curriculum intentions with investigations and explanations.

KTT 9: Predictions, measurements, worksheet responses and questioning inform checks of understanding.

KTT 10: Mentor and visitor feedback evaluate my response to behaviour and the management of practical learning.

KTT 11: Visual guides, scaffolded instructions and responsive support provide different ways for learners to participate.

Year 10 Science 2 completed Lesson 4, the current-measurement practical, on 29 October 2026. Most students worked productively; two students did not complete the task despite reminders. This observation helps identify where more individual checking is needed.
