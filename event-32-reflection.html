Professional Experience 1

Astronomy exam preparation and rolling investigation: Reflection

Year 11 Science · 23/10/2026

This lesson was more difficult to manage than the Year 12 and Year 13 sessions. There were 25 students, and I would estimate that about half struggled to stay on task. A relieving teacher was present and helped, but maintaining participation was still difficult. I felt that the size of the class contributed to the challenge. my mentor teacher had advised me to focus on supporting students rather than taking a strict disciplinary role, so I followed that approach for the most part. I went to students who were not working and offered help. Some returned to the task, but others remained off task despite the efforts of both the relieving teacher and me.
