Lesson 06: Reliable connections and switched outlets

Science Curriculum Links

Conceptual Focus Statement

Physical systems involve interactions between matter, fields, and the transfer of energy.

Learning Focus

Learning Aims

Teaching/Pedagogical Approaches

The arcade clip and my experience working in a gaming arcade will introduce the relevance of reliable connections. I will demonstrate the measurements and graph, with the students interpreting the evidence. The familiar household fitting gives a purpose to the circuit work. I will demonstrate the connection and inspection process using the same fitting as the students, then provide support at the point of difficulty. An annotated example, vocabulary prompts and a first-connection check will make the starting point clearer. I will reduce this support as the students become more independent, and offer a disconnected fault diagnosis to those who finish early.

Assessment Opportunities/Methods

I will use the opening fault suggestions to assess prior reasoning. During construction, I will check each learner’s first connection and ask them to trace a selected path. Inspection records, disconnected continuity results, lamp tests and individual exit responses will provide evidence of understanding. A working lamp will show that the assembly functions, but will not by itself establish what each partner understands.

Teaching and Learning Sequence

Duration: 60 minutes
