Week 7 Reflection

9–13 November 2026

This reflection follows Rolfe et al.’s (2001) “What? So what? Now what?” framework.

What?

This week, my Year 10 classes moved towards electricity generation. After no group in one class completed the two-lamp challenge in about forty minutes, I discussed support with my mentor teacher and drew a colour-coded circuit guide on card. When I revisited the task as a class competition, almost every group finished within ten to fifteen minutes.

At the generator stations, students observed changes in voltage as they moved a magnet or spun a motor, then offered explanations. Science 1 later built six turbine models and explained possible improvements.

So what?

The guide connected the physical components with their circuit representation without making the connections for students. This resembles scaffolding that helps learners approach a task while retaining responsibility for it (Wood et al., 1976). However, the class and task introduction also differed, so I cannot attribute improved completion to the guide alone.

My mentor teacher encouraged a more theatrical opening to generate curiosity. I introduced a question, gave students time to investigate, then developed their explanations into a discussion of power stations. He said the way I used their curiosity was something he would take away. This helped me see how teacher talk can invite thinking rather than provide every answer.

Completion was encouraging; my next question is whether students can explain and reproduce the circuit without the guide.

Now what?

During the field trip, students will test their turbines and use observations to justify what they would retain or change, including when a design performs poorly. In future practical lessons, I will provide a clear starting point, gradually reduce support and collect individual explanations alongside group products.

References

Wood, D., Bruner, J. S., & Ross, G. (1976). The role of tutoring in problem solving. Journal of Child Psychology and Psychiatry, 17(2), 89–100. https://doi.org/10.1111/j.1469-7610.1976.tb00381.x
