Lesson 07: Independently switched parallel lamps

Science Curriculum Links

Conceptual Focus Statement

Physical systems involve interactions between matter, fields, and the transfer of energy.

Learning Focus

Learning Aims

Teaching/Pedagogical Approaches

I will begin with a diagram challenge so that the students commit to an idea before seeing the complete construction diagram. They will then build, test and troubleshoot in pairs. An annotated branch diagram and staged connection checks will support those who need help, while early finishers will trace the four paths or diagnose a disconnected fault. I will give the closing demonstration only after practical explanations and equipment pack-up.

Assessment Opportunities/Methods

I will assess each learner’s construction and checking, the four-state results table and individual path explanations. The practical review will ask what happens to the other lamp when one switch changes. Short responses during the safety discussion will provide an initial check of the different functions of MCBs and RCCBs.

Teaching and Learning Sequence

Duration: 60 minutes
