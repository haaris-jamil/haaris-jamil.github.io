What the retained student work supports

Lesson 4 current investigation

The five exercise-book pages are dated 23 October and support Science 1, not Science 2. Page 2 contains a prediction that readings would differ, followed by the learner's statement that they were wrong and the readings were the same. Page 3 records comparable readings at the three positions and explicitly distinguishes the lamp's energy transfer from charge. These support conceptual revision for those samples.

There are also useful limitations. Page 2 defines current as energy, so the vocabulary remains insecure despite a correct practical pattern. Several numerical entries lack clear units or need checking against the instrument setting. Page 4 has a questionable extra circuit path in the drawing and slightly different numerical readings. Ask the learner to explain the drawn path and interpret their measurements rather than silently correcting the scan. Five selected pages cannot establish whole-class mastery.
