Student Voice

28 students responded across both Year 10 Science groups. This combines 18 earlier forms with ten forms from Science 1 on 18 November 2026. All ten students present on 18 November responded. The questions ask about learning during the placement.

All 28 respondents agreed or strongly agreed that they received the help they needed when they struggled. 25 of 28 reported improved understanding of circuits, and 23 of 28 rated the models and demonstrations positively. 22 of 28 reported enough challenge, and 23 of 28 felt comfortable asking questions, making mistakes and sharing ideas. One student strongly disagreed with each of the last two statements.

Neutral means neither agree nor disagree. Where two ratings were marked, the lower rating was used.

Written comments across the forms describe helpful explanations, demonstrations and opportunities to ask questions. Two earlier respondents wrote that I gave them mana. New suggestions included allowing time to finish, breaking lessons into manageable parts and providing notes students could return to after an absence. Several students gave no suggestion for change.

The models and demonstrations received positive ratings from 13 of 18 earlier respondents and ten of ten later respondents. These are small samples collected at different times, and the later group had very low attendance. The figures describe these respondents’ experiences and do not establish a class-wide difference in teaching effectiveness.

I want to retain clear explanations and responsive support while checking that tasks provide enough challenge and that every student feels comfortable contributing. Students’ responses inform this reflection alongside their classroom work.
