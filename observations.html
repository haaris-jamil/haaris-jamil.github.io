Student Voice

18 of 18 students responded in one Year 10 science class. All 18 said they received the help they needed when they struggled, and 17 said the lessons helped them understand circuits.

Two students wrote that I gave them mana. Their words stayed with me and made me reflect on how I can support students without taking over. In a separate response, one student described being shown what to do and then having the chance to do it themselves.

The responses about models and demonstrations were more mixed. In my next placement, I want to ask students to explain what a model helped them understand and allow more time to finish the main practical task, with an extension ready for those who finish early. This is feedback about students’ experiences and perceived learning, alongside the evidence of what they produced in class.
