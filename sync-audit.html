2. Files not set to sync automatically

No individual source files inside the portfolio folder have deliberately been excluded from the new folder-wide scanner. Until its first successful full run, however, the 79 linked files are not yet being copied as native website content. The five weekly reflections are already published as webpages.

When the extended workflow operates, supported Docs, Sheets, Slides and stored files up to 40 MB will be mirrored. Larger files will remain linked to Google Drive. Links to files outside the portfolio folder and interactive elements such as spreadsheet macros or slide animations are not copied automatically.
