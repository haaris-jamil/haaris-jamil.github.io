Week 5 Reflection

26–30 October 2026 | Placement Week 5

This reflection follows Rolfe et al.’s (2001) “What? So what? Now what?” framework.

What?

This week, the main challenge was responding to different starting points while keeping the lessons moving. On Wednesday, the crossword revealed gaps in understanding, so I spent fifteen minutes revisiting earlier ideas. Students collected circuit readings, but graphing carried forward. On Thursday, most students worked productively, although I could not get two students to complete the task despite repeated reminders. On Friday, six boys who had missed the previous lesson needed substantial support. I worked through the experiment with them, then changed the planned resistance investigation into a demonstration to accommodate the remaining time.

So what?

These lessons reminded me that responding to learners involves more than adjusting timings. A recap can be worthwhile even when it delays the planned activity, but catching up one group can also reduce opportunities for everyone else. I think Friday’s discussion established useful connections between voltage, current and resistance, although this does not establish every student’s understanding. Repeated reminders were also insufficient for some students. my mentor’s perspective on Thursday helped me recognise a positive in the interactions without overlooking the unfinished learning. I need to distinguish maintaining a relationship, securing participation and establishing understanding rather than treating them as the same outcome.

Now what?

I will prepare a short re-entry task for students who have missed a practical and agree with my mentor how to support those who remain off task. I will retain brief individual checks so that class discussion is not my only evidence of understanding. I also want students to complete the hands-on resistance investigation, rather than leave the demonstration as their only experience. I will record these adaptations in the lesson plans while preserving the original unit plan. my mentor’s formal feedback on Friday’s lesson is pending and will inform the next review.

References

Rolfe, G., Freshwater, D., & Jasper, M. (2001). Critical reflection for nursing and the helping professions: A user’s guide. Palgrave.
