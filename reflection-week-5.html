Week 5 Reflection

26–30 October 2026 | Placement Week 5

This reflection follows Rolfe et al.’s (2001) “What? So what? Now what?” framework.

What?

This week, I adjusted lessons for different starting points. Wednesday’s crossword revealed gaps, so I spent fifteen minutes revisiting earlier ideas. Students collected circuit readings, but graphing carried forward. On Thursday, most worked productively, although two did not complete the task despite reminders. On Friday, six students who had missed the previous lesson needed substantial support. I worked through the experiment with them and changed the planned resistance investigation into a demonstration because time was short.

So what?

Responding to learners involves more than changing timings. Reteaching can provide a necessary foundation, but supporting an absent group can reduce opportunities for others. I felt Friday’s discussion connected voltage, current and resistance, although this did not establish every student’s understanding.

Black and Wiliam’s (2009) account of formative assessment helps me interpret the crossword as evidence that changed my teaching. I still need individual responses after the demonstration, rather than rely on those who speak. My mentor teacher’s perspective also helped me distinguish maintaining a relationship, securing participation and establishing understanding. These are related, but one does not guarantee the others.

Now what?

I will prepare a short re-entry task for students who miss a practical and discuss how to follow up when reminders are ineffective. I will retain individual checks and give students an opportunity to complete the hands-on resistance investigation. I will document adaptations while preserving the original unit plan. At this point, Friday’s formal mentor feedback is pending and will inform my next review.

References

Black, P., & Wiliam, D. (2009). Developing the theory of formative assessment. Educational Assessment, Evaluation and Accountability, 21(1), 5–31. https://doi.org/10.1007/s11092-008-9068-5

Rolfe, G., Freshwater, D., & Jasper, M. (2001). Critical reflection for nursing and the helping professions: A user’s guide. Palgrave.
