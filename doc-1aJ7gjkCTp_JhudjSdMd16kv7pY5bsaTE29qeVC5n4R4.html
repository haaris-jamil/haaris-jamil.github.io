Professional Experience 1

Lesson 10: Electrical energy and efficiency

Year 10 Science 1 · 13 Nov 2026 · Planned

Year 10 Science | 55 minutes

Learning intention

Build and test an electromagnet. Use observations to explain what happens when current flows through a coil.

Success criteria

Build a coil around a nail, compare what happens with the power off and on, and use a fair test to compare two numbers of turns.

Preparation

Pre-test the setup with the two agreed turn counts. Prepare identical iron nails, equal lengths of insulated copper wire with ends prepared by the teacher, identical steel paper clips and current-limited low-voltage supplies. Set the approved current before students connect. Use the same wire and current for both coils and verify a clear off/on result. Check connections before energising. Use brief trials, switch off between tests and disconnect before handling or rewinding. If the turn comparison is unclear, repeat the off/on test and compare results across pairs.

Teaching sequence

Teacher activity

Student activity

Recall | 5 min. Ask what moved in Lesson 09 and what the meter did. Pose the new question: Can a wire and a nail pick up steel clips?

Tell a partner one thing you saw in Lesson 09. Predict how many clips the nail might lift.

Build | 15 min. Hand out supplies and show how to wind the wire. Check each coil before connecting the supply.

Wind the insulated wire snugly around the nail. Count the turns. Ask for a connection check.

Test | 10 min. Supervise the brief off/on comparison and ask pairs what changed.

Count the clips the nail lifts with the power off, then on. Switch off and discuss what you saw.

Compare | 15 min. Give each pair two agreed turn counts. Keep current, nail, wire and lifting method consistent.

Disconnect before rewinding. Test both turn counts and repeat each test. Record the number of clips lifted.

Plenary | 10 min. Hear two pairs’ findings. Ask for one observation from each lesson. Clarify the link in under a minute. Collect individual exit responses.

Use your results to explain what changed when the power came on. Compare the coil here with the magnet and coil from Lesson 09. Write one result that supports your explanation.
