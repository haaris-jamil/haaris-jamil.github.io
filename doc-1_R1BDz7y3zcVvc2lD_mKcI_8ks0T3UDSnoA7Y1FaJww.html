Planning and evidence conventions

Use “Lesson XX: Topic - Y10 Science 1/2 - Full Lesson Plan”. Maintain one current plan for each lesson in each class folder, 24 current plans in total. Use the class-specific plan linked in the register. Preserve historical delivery accounts and distinguish them from later planned refinements. Retain shared slides and worksheets where suitable.

Full plans include verbatim curriculum statements, learning intentions and success criteria, resources and safety, a timed teaching sequence with separate teacher and student actions, assessment and differentiation, an explicit plenary, and post-lesson reflection. Three highlighted plans demonstrate this approach; they do not replace the remaining plans.

Post-lesson reflections belong at the end of the plan for that class and delivery. Formal mentor observation and feedback records remain separate and unaltered. Link each event to its class-specific plan, retaining the distinction between planned dates, completed delivery and sessions not delivered by me. Science 1 Lesson 02 was not delivered by me on 16 October; Science 2 Lesson 11 and Lesson 12 dates remain TBC.
