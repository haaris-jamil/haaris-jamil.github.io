Planning and evidence conventions

Use “Lesson XX: Topic - Y10 Science 1/2 - Full Lesson Plan”. Retain only lesson plans and slides linked in the Learning Events Register. Use the class-specific resources and preserve historical delivery accounts, distinguishing them from later planned refinements. Retain shared worksheets where suitable.

Full plans include verbatim curriculum statements, learning intentions and success criteria, resources and safety, a timed teaching sequence with separate teacher and student actions, assessment and differentiation, an explicit plenary, and post-lesson reflection. Three highlighted plans demonstrate this approach; they do not replace the remaining plans.

Post-lesson reflections belong at the end of the plan for that class and delivery. Formal mentor observation and feedback records remain separate and unaltered. Link each event to its class-specific plan, retaining the distinction between completed delivery and sessions not delivered by me. Science 1 Lesson 02 was not delivered by me on 16 October.
